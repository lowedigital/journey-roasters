Our Story

Roasted with intention, made for the journey.

Journey Roasters began with a simple belief: great coffee should be honest, unhurried, and made with care from the first sort to the last drop. Every batch is small, roasted in limited quantities so we can taste, adjust, and hold a standard that never wavers.

We work with select origins — from the deep, dark roasts of Brazil to the bright, balanced cups of Colombia — roasting each one with intention to bring out its own character. Every bag is consistent, grounding, and good company for wherever the day takes you.

Brewing

Simple ways to brew it well.

Pour Over

  1. Use a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio, medium-fine grind.
  2. Bloom grounds for 30 seconds with hot water.
  3. Pour in slow circles over 3 minutes.

French Press

  1. Coarse grind, 1:15 ratio with just-off-boil water.
  2. Steep for 4 minutes, undisturbed.
  3. Plunge slowly and serve immediately.

Drip Coffee

  1. Medium grind, 1:17 ratio for a balanced cup.
  2. Use filtered water for the cleanest flavor.
  3. Brew and serve fresh within 30 minutes.
